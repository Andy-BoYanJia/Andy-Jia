HOW CAN I HELP?

Learn with me.

Want to try a sport, but need someone to start with? Join me for football or frisbee. We can practice, share what we know and learn together.

01 / FOOTBALL

Your practice partner.

For classmates who want to play football and need someone to practice with. Let's work on short passes, receiving the ball and simple teamwork.

I play midfield for my school football team. I'd like to share what I learn in training and keep improving with you.

View activity details

What we can do

Start with a warm-up, practice short passes and first touches, then try a simple passing challenge together. We can adjust the practice to your experience.

Suggested session

About 30 minutes, with the time and school pitch availability agreed together beforehand. This is a proposed plan, with no fixed weekly schedule yet.

How to start

Find me at school and say, “I'd like to practice football with you.” Tell me what you want to try and when you're free. We'll confirm a time and place together.

02 / FRISBEE

Your first throws.

For classmates who are curious about frisbee and haven't tried it yet. We can explore the basic rules and practice short throws and catches together.

I started playing in sixth grade and took part in a school-team tournament in 2023. I'd like to help you feel comfortable joining in.

View activity details

What we can do

Go over the basic rules, try short-distance throws and catches, and practice passing with a partner. If more classmates join, we can try a small group activity.

Suggested session

About 30 minutes. We'll agree on a time and a suitable open space at school, and check that a disc is available. This is a proposed plan, with no fixed weekly schedule yet.

How to start

Find me at school and say, “I'd like to try frisbee.” No experience is needed. Let me know when you're free so we can arrange a first practice.
